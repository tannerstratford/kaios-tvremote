Trust the Vizio TV

The TV Remote app sees your Vizio but marks it unverified — SmartCast uses HTTPS with a self-signed cert the phone doesn't trust. This registers a cert override for that one TV (192.168.1.174:7345), the same way the browser's "add exception" does.

Step 2 — on the blank System page

Type these two commands into the address bar, in order, in the same tab:

javascript:document.createElement('script') javascript:document.write(name)

A black bar shows the result. TRUST OK means done — reopen TV Remote and scan. Copy any TRUST FAILED text back.
